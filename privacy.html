Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

This site does not collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, and no cookies set for tracking. Nothing you do here is profiled or sold.

If you contact me

The contact form opens your own email application so you can send the message yourself. If your browser cannot do that, the form falls back to a third-party delivery service that forwards the message to my inbox and does not retain it.

Either way, I only receive what you choose to write: your name, your email address, and your message. I use it to reply to you, nothing else. I do not add you to a mailing list and I do not share it.

Your choices

Since nothing is stored, there is no account to delete and no data to export. If you have emailed me and want your message deleted, ask and I will remove it.
